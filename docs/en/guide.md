# Using Hearth

[Deutsch](../de/anleitung.md) · [All docs](../README.md)

## Menus

- **Server name** at the top of the channel list: invite people, server settings, create channels and categories, copy the server ID, leave.
- **Right-click a member** (member list, name or avatar in chat, voice channel): profile, mention, send a message, nickname, roles with checkboxes, moderator view, timeout, kick, ban, copy user ID. Only actions you are allowed to do appear.
- **Right-click a message**: react, start a thread, edit, copy text, mention the author, copy the message ID, delete.
- **Right-click a channel or category**: mark as read, edit, create a channel inside, copy the ID.

## Moderator view

Opens from a member's menu for anyone with a moderation permission. It shows the member's message, link and media counts on this server, audit log entries about them, their effective permissions, their roles (add and remove right there), registration and join dates and a running timeout.

## Messages

Markdown: `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `~~struck~~`, `` `code` `` and code blocks. Mention people with `@username` and everyone with `@everyone`; messages that mention you are highlighted. The emoji button has a search that understands English and German words, for example `fire` or `feuer`.

Threads start from a message (right-click or the thread button) and show up under their channel. Polls are created with the poll button next to the message field, with one or several answers and an optional end time.

## Voice and screen sharing

Click a voice channel to join. Screen shares appear as tiles; you start watching by clicking one, and the sharer sees who watches. Sounds play when people join, leave and start or stop sharing.

## Profiles and badges

Profiles have avatar, banner, bio, accent colour, activity and linked accounts. Badges come in two kinds: badges the instance admin gives (no description needed), and earned badges for playtime, stream time, voice time and account age in ten colour levels. Click an earned badge to see your progress.

## Status

Click your own avatar to pick a status:

- **Online**
- **Idle**, also set automatically after 10 minutes without input and cleared as soon as you are back
- **Do not disturb**: no sounds for messages and calls
- **Invisible**: others see you as offline and without activity, you can still use everything

The status is saved and stays after a restart.

## Friends and groups

Add friends by username. Direct messages work between friends and between members of a shared server. Group chats hold up to 10 people with a picture, roles and calls. Blocked users cannot message or call you.

## Language and theme

In Settings, **Appearance** picks the theme (Light, Ash, Dark, Onyx, Midnight, Parchment) and **Language** switches between German, English and Danish.
